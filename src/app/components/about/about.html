# Whoami

Hi, I'm Danyal Ahmed. I am a Lead Full-Stack and Backend Software Engineer dedicated to building robust, enterprise-grade applications and complex microservices.

My engineering foundation started with a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science from Sindh Madressatul Islam University. Over the years, my focus has gravitated heavily toward backend architecture. I specialize in Laravel, Node.js, and NestJS, orchestrating data across relational and search-optimized databases like MySQL, Oracle, and Elasticsearch. On the frontend, I rely on modern React and strict, clean vanilla JavaScript—you won't find a single line of jQuery in my recent deployments.

Lately, my work intersects directly with applied AI. For the competitive intelligence platform DealSense, I engineered a system leveraging Large Language Models (LLMs) to instantly parse unstructured credit agreements into structured financial statistics and dashboards. I have also integrated the Google Vision API to automate visual defect detection for multi-tenant field audit portals at Pakistan State Oil.

I love the rapid prototyping phase of software. I frequently partner with a dedicated frontend developer friend to evaluate and launch new SaaS ideas, utilizing workflow orchestration tools like n8n and Lovable to move from concept to production quickly.

Beyond the codebase, my life is anchored by my family and my kids. To unwind, I lean into my creative side—directing and producing AI-generated cinematic short videos and skydiving animations for YouTube using tools like Google Veo and Kling AI. All of this is, of course, fueled by an ongoing supply of Nescafé instant tea.
