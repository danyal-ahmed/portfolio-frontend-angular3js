01 The Longer Version

A bit about me

I 'm a full-stack software engineer and lead backend developer based in Karachi. My path into software is rooted in building scalable, enterprise-grade systems. Working with frameworks like Laravel and NestJS, and orchestrating complex databases, I discovered early on that writing clean, functional code that powers real-world business operations is what drives me today.

I studied Computer Science at Sindh Madressatul Islam University, where I learned just how much the fundamentals matter. Algorithms, data structures, and the reasoning behind them never go out of style. Frameworks change every year, but understanding why things work lets you choose the right approach. My professional journey has spanned building multi-tenant platforms for Pakistan State Oil to engineering financial analytics engines for DealSense. Most recently, I wrapped up a role at TechSurge, where I kept growing and proving my expertise across the whole stack.

Lately, I've been focused on staying current with where the industry is heading, spending a lot of time experimenting with large language models, OpenAI integrations, and workflow orchestrators like n8n. This portfolio is part of that: I treat it as a playground to showcase what these tools can do once they are wired into a real product.

When I'm away from the keyboard, I'm usually exploring the creative side of technology. I spend time creating AI-generated animated short videos and cinematic clips using tools like Google Veo and Kling AI for YouTube. And last but not least, I'm completely addicted to a good cup of instant tea from my Nescafé machine while brainstorming the next SaaS idea with my friends.
